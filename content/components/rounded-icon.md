# Rounded icon

Rounded icons show an icon on a colored circle, to give a message or an item a type or a color.

Code: `NeRoundedIcon` · [Storybook](https://nethesis.github.io/vue-components/?path=/docs/components-neroundedicon--docs) · [Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/ZRZzzlqb9GfB9Xdz6iGufh/NDS?node-id=177-62277)

## Variants

- **Status:** in [modals](?page=modal) and notifications, with four types: information, success, warning and error.
- **Color:** in tables and other components, next to icons, such as the color of a network zone.

The icon is 20 px.
